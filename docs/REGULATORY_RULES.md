# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 - Extracted Rules

*Note: This document contains versioned rules that the deterministic rule engine will enforce.*

## Version 1.0 (MVP)

### Rule 1: MRP Declaration (Rule 6(1)(e))
- **Requirement**: Every package must bear the Retail Sale Price of the package.
- **Keywords/Patterns**: "MRP", "Max Retail Price", "Rs.", "₹"
- **Condition**: Must be present and legible.

### Rule 2: Net Quantity (Rule 6(1)(c))
- **Requirement**: Every package must bear the net quantity by weight, measure, or number.
- **Keywords/Patterns**: "Net Weight", "Net Vol", "Net Qty", "g", "kg", "ml", "L"
- **Condition**: Must be present.

### Rule 3: Manufacturer / Packer Details (Rule 6(1)(a))
- **Requirement**: Name and address of the manufacturer, or packer, or importer.
- **Keywords/Patterns**: "Mfd. by", "Packed by", "Manufactured By", "Imported By", "Address:"
- **Condition**: Must be present.

### Rule 4: Month and Year of Manufacture/Packing (Rule 6(1)(d))
- **Requirement**: Month and year of manufacture or pre-packing or import.
- **Keywords/Patterns**: "Mfd. Month", "Pkd.", "Mfg. Date", "Date of Packing"
- **Condition**: Must be present.
